Your task is to generate a **deep, system-level product specification document** for a platform called **MetaLingo**.

This is NOT a marketing document.
This is NOT a feature list.

This must be a **complete ecosystem-level specification** that explains how all parts of the platform interact, influence each other, and create value.

---

# 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Produce a document that allows:

* A product designer to design the full UX
* A developer to understand system behavior
* A marketer to extract real differentiators

---

# 🧩 CORE PRINCIPLE

MetaLingo is NOT a “course platform”.

It is a **multi-layered learning ecosystem** combining:

* Education (courses, private classes)
* Marketplace (CallerN tutors)
* AI system (real-time + analytical)
* CRM (lead → enrollment pipeline)
* Engagement systems (gamification, referrals)
* Content & SEO engine

👉 You must explain how these layers CONNECT.
